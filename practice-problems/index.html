Easy DSA Problems for Beginners — Starter Set by Topic

Every problem below is from a free online judge (mostly LeetCode). We don't host or reproduce problems — we link to them so you can solve them on the source platform. Each problem is labelled with a difficulty level and the pattern it teaches.

Honesty note: Difficulty labels (easy/medium) are our own subjective assessment. "Easy" = approachable if you just finished reading about the topic. "Medium" = you may need to think longer or look up the pattern. Your mileage may vary.

How to use this page: Follow the DSA Roadmap order. After reading the resource for a topic, come here and solve its starter problems. Don't jump ahead — each section assumes knowledge from earlier ones.
